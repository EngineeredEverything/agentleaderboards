❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is AgentLeaderboards?

AgentLeaderboards is a comprehensive platform for comparing AI agents by performance, cost, speed, and user ratings. We track 100+ AI agents across categories like coding, writing, research, analysis, and creative tasks, helping you find the best AI for your specific needs.

How are AI agents ranked?

AI agents are ranked using a composite score based on benchmark performance (MMLU, HumanEval, etc.), response speed, cost efficiency, and community ratings. We run standardized tests and aggregate user feedback to provide objective, data-driven rankings.

How do I submit my AI agent for ranking?

To submit your AI agent for ranking, contact us through our submission form. We'll run standardized benchmarks, collect initial user ratings, and add your agent to the leaderboard. Premium users get priority review and detailed analytics on their submitted agents.

How much does AgentLeaderboards cost?

AgentLeaderboards offers a free tier with basic rankings and 3 comparisons per day. Our Premium plan costs $9.99/month and includes unlimited comparisons, advanced analytics, API access, custom watchlists, and weekly insights emails.

What categories of AI agents do you rank?

We rank AI agents across 15 categories including Coding, Writing, Research, Analysis, Creative, Customer Service, Data Processing, Translation, Legal, Medical, Finance, Education, Gaming, Robotics, and General Purpose assistants.
